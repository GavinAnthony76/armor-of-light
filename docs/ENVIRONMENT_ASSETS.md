# Environment artwork provenance and prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for the game. No CLI/API-key fallback. Format conversion preserves alpha; no original art was overwritten. Outputs are consumed through the existing asset resolver and included in static/standalone builds.

1. `public/assets/desert-sky-v2.webp`
Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game background asset for Armor of Light, a mobile 2D side-scrolling illustrated fantasy adventure through ancient desert ruins. WIDE landscape 1536x1024. ONLY distant environment: warm amber sunset sky, layered dusty mountains, far-off tiny sandstone city silhouettes in lower quarter, wispy drifting cloud banks. Painterly polished hand-painted videogame art, rich but restrained colors, atmospheric perspective, clear simple value masses readable on phone. Keep middle and lower-middle open for character silhouettes. No nearby pillars, no foreground, no playable floor, no characters, no weapons, no text, no UI, no watermark. This is the far background plate of a multi-layer parallax scene; objects must remain distant and low contrast. Avoid realistic photo rendering.

No characters, text, UI, symbols, watermarks. Top third entirely transparent; gaps between columns and arches transparent; this layer scrolls separately over a sunset sky.
