---
name: Starter audio approval
description: Scope of the user's permission to use the supplied synthesized starter sound pack.
---

The user chose **“Use suitable starter effects”** when offered that pack,
alternative recordings, or the current synthesized sounds.
This is approval to select suitable effects, not blanket approval of all
starter audio or its draft music loops.

**Why:** The starter pack had no sound approval until the user made this choice;
uploading a mixed art/audio archive alone was not approval to install everything.

**How to apply:** Keep selection restricted to matching existing SFX events.
Do not invent footsteps, checkpoint cues or ambience triggers to use unused files.
Retain the adaptive synthesized score and immediate fallback. Do not represent
starter approval as human listening or physical-phone certification.
